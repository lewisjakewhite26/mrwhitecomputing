VIDEO PRODUCTION · LESSON 4

Make a film trailer

Today your group plans a trailer, makes a shot list, then goes out and films every shot. Editing comes next lesson.

What is a trailer?

BEAT 1

Hook

A surprise or a question that grabs you.

BEAT 2

Story

Who it is about, and what goes wrong.

BEAT 3

Build

Shots get quicker. The tension rises.

BEAT 4

Title

The name of the film, on a title card.

A trailer shows just enough to make people want more. It never gives away the ending.

Pick your genre

AdventureWide shots, running, a map
ComedySilly faces and a big surprise
MysteryClose-ups, shadows, a clue
SpookySlow shots, quiet, then a bang

Groups of three. Agree one genre in two minutes. Your own genre is fine too.

Filming rules

Hold it wideLandscape, like a cinema screen
Hold it stillTwo hands, elbows in, count to five at the end
Film three takesSame shot, three goes. Pick the best later
Quiet on setCount down 3, 2, 1. Everyone else is silent
Only your groupNobody else is in your shots
Agreed places onlyStay where your teacher said you could film

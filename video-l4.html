What is a trailer?

1

Hook

Something that makes you look up, like a question or a sudden bang.

2

Story

Who we're following, and what has gone wrong for them.

3

Build

The shots get shorter while the music climbs.

4

Title

The film's name on a black card, usually right at the end.

A trailer gives away just enough. Show the ending and nobody needs to watch the film.

Filming rules

Hold it wideLandscape, like a cinema screen
Hold it stillElbows in, and keep filming for five seconds after the action ends
Film three takesThe first go is rarely the best, so choose later
Quiet on setCount 3, 2, 1 on your fingers; a spoken count ends up on the audio
Only your groupNobody else wanders into frame
Agreed places onlyStay where your teacher said you could go

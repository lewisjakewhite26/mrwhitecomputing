Planning an animation

Before anyone takes a photo, you decide what happens, how many photos it needs and whether you can finish in time.

What goes wrong with no plan?

Your film needs 200 photos and you only have time for 50.
Halfway through, nobody remembers what happens next.
You and your partner each picture a different film.
A plan stops all three.

How many photos is a film?

FILM LENGTH
5seconds
1 second of film plays 10 photos.
5 × 10 = 50 photos

Plan your Roman animation

1Pick an idea: a soldier marches, a ship sails in, a wall builds itself, or your own
2Draw four to six panels on your sheet
3In each panel, write what moves, what stays the same and how many photos
4Add up your photos. Aim for about 50

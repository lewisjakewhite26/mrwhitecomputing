Two words for today

animation

Lots of pictures shown quickly, one after another, so they look like they are moving.

frame

One of the pictures in an animation. Our bouncing ball had eight frames.

The order matters. Mixed up frames make a muddle.

The thaumatrope

A bird on one side of a card. A cage on the other. Spin it and see what happens.

Spin it fast and your eyes put the two pictures together. The bird is in the cage.

Say it: THAW-ma-trope. It comes from two Greek words that mean wonder and turning.

Which one will work?

Card A
It works. The bird is in the middle, like the cage.
Card B
The bird is outside. It was drawn too near the edge.

Which would make a good thaumatrope?

Make your own thaumatrope

1Choose two pictures that go together.
2Draw one picture in the middle of each circle.
3Tape a straw to the back of one circle.
4Stick the circles back to back, both the right way up.
5Roll the straw fast between your hands.

YOU NEED

  • 2 card circles
  • Pencils and colours
  • A straw or a pencil
  • Tape or a glue stick

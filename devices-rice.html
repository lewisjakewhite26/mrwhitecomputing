1 grain of rice = 1 device

How we worked it out

  • 33 billion connected devices: Statista's figures for October 2025 add up to about 33.4 billion: 17.4 billion short-range smart devices (speakers, watches, trackers), 8.6 billion mobile phones, 4.9 billion wide-area devices, 1.7 billion computers and 0.8 billion landlines.
  • About 4 each: 33 billion ÷ about 8.2 billion people is about 4 devices per person, on average around the world.
  • Stockton: the borough has about 200,000 people (2021 Census: 196,600). Using the world average of about 4 each gives roughly 800,000 devices. It's an estimate, not a count.
  • One grain of long-grain rice weighs about 0.02 g, so a 1 kg bag holds roughly 50,000 grains. 800,000 ÷ 50,000 = 16 bags.
  • 33 billion grains ÷ 50,000 = 660,000 kg, or 660 tonnes. An empty double-decker bus weighs about 12 tonnes, so that is about 55 buses.
  • Rice packs at about 800 kg per cubic metre, so 660 tonnes takes up about 825 m³. A small house with four 3 m × 3 m rooms holds about 90 m³, so that is about 9 houses.
  • Checking one per second, non-stop: 800,000 seconds is about 9 days. 33 billion seconds is about 1,046 years.

The jobs a video can do

Video is moving pictures, usually with sound, recorded so people can watch them later or somewhere else.

Inform

The news, the weather, a how-to video on YouTube

Entertain

Films, cartoons, a funny Short

Promote

Adverts, film trailers, and music videos too

Document

A wedding video, the school play, a record of wild animals

Plenty of videos do two jobs at once. A music video entertains you and sells the song.

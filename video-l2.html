Shot size: how much do we see?

Wide shot

The whole place, with people small inside it
Shows where we are

Mid shot

A person from the waist up
Shows who and what they’re doing

Close-up

Just a face, or one small thing
Shows how someone feels
News presenters are usually filmed in a mid shot. Next time you watch the news, check.

One camera operator sat back on a long lens for the tight shots, far enough away not to frighten anything.
Two more ran beside the iguanas with handheld cameras on steadying rigs.
For Dynasties, the hyena crew used a lens that zooms 20 times, from 50mm to 1000mm, so they could film from a long way off.

Why your iPad can’t zoom like the BBC

Zoom1×
A photo is made of pixels. Pinching just makes each pixel bigger, so the picture goes blocky and blurry. A real lens, or your own feet, collects more detail.

Filming on an iPad

Turn it sidewaysVideos are wide, so hold the iPad the same way
Two hands, elbows inTuck your elbows against your body to stop the wobble
Count to fivePress record, count five, do the shot, count five, then stop
Walk, don’t pinchMove the iPad closer for a close-up instead of zooming
Move slowlyPan and tilt slower than feels right
Film the toys, not facesHands and toys are fine; keep people’s faces out

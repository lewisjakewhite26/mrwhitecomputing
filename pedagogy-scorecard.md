# Pedagogy scorecard: before vs after, all 18 lessons

All 18 lessons are now built. This replaces the earlier "current vs
projected" version with an honest before/after: what the lesson scored
before this work started, and what it actually scores now that the build
is done, not what was originally planned. Several builds turned out
differently from the plan in `enrichment-research.md`, mostly smaller and
more homegrown than the full external resource, and the scores below
reflect what's actually live in `index.html`, not the ambition.

## How the score works

Same bands as before, still a judgement call:

| Band | What it means |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | A checked, hands-on activity is embedded in the deck itself, reveal-card load is low, pupils get real feedback on whether they're right |
| 60–79 | A checked activity exists, but it's either a late addition rather than core to the lesson, or a strong external resource with its own built-in check |
| 40–59 | Something exists for pupils to do: a worksheet, a demo, a printed sort. It isn't checked or paced inside the deck |
| 20–39 | Mostly or entirely tap-to-reveal cards. The real work is a printed sheet the deck never touches |

## Overall

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **All 18 lessons, average** | **42.2 / 100** | **75.7 / 100** |

Every lesson in the curriculum now has at least one checked, hands-on
activity in the deck itself. Fourteen of eighteen had none before this
work. The after-scores land a little under the original projections in
`enrichment-research.md` in a handful of lessons (Y3/4 L3, L4, L6; Y5/6 L4,
L5), because the actual builds are homegrown checked activities rather than
the full external resources those projections assumed. That's a fair
trade: every one of those lessons still gained a real, working, checked
activity where it had nothing before. None of the builds fall short of
"something checked now exists"; they just don't all match the single best
possible resource that was scouted for them.

# Pedagogy scorecard: current structure vs planned changes, all 18 lessons

This sits alongside `pedagogy-audit.md` (the structural counts) and
`enrichment-research.md` (the sourced replacements). It turns both into one
number per lesson: where it stands now, and where it lands if the planned
changes are built.

## How the score works

Not a formula — a judgement call, same basis as the resource scores in
`enrichment-research.md`. Each lesson is placed in a band based on what a
pupil actually *does* in the deck, not just what the deck *tells* them:

| Band | What it means |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | A checked, hands-on activity is embedded in the deck itself, reveal-card load is low, pupils get real feedback on whether they're right |
| 60–79 | A checked activity exists, but either it's a late addition rather than core to the lesson, or it's a strong external resource swapped in with its own built-in check |
| 40–59 | Something exists for pupils to do (a worksheet, a demo, a printed sort) but it isn't checked or paced inside the deck |
| 20–39 | Mostly or entirely tap-to-reveal cards; the real work is a printed sheet the deck never touches |

Current-state numbers are read directly off the counts in
`pedagogy-audit.md` (slides, reveal cards, whether a Check-button activity
already exists). Projected numbers factor in the specific resource or build
recommended for that lesson in `enrichment-research.md`, weighted down from
that resource's own score where slotting it in doesn't solve the *whole*
lesson's problem (an honest gap stays a gap, and still costs points).

## Overall

| | Current | Projected |
|---|---|---|
| **All 18 lessons, average** | **42.2 / 100** | **76.9 / 100** |

The gap isn't the content — the audit already found the factual content and
the worksheets themselves are fine. It's that fourteen of eighteen lessons
never check inside the deck whether a pupil did the thinking, they only tell
them the answer and move on. Every planned change above either embeds a
checked activity the lesson currently lacks, or replaces a
watch-only demo with something a pupil has to actually do and get feedback
on. Two lessons (Y1/2 L5, Y3/4 L3) still land under 70 projected — both are
flagged honestly in `enrichment-research.md` as places where no strong free
resource exists, so the projected score reflects a partial fix, not a
solved one.

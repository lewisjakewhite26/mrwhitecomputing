# Pedagogy scorecard: current structure vs planned changes, all 18 lessons

This sits alongside `pedagogy-audit.md` (the structural counts) and
`enrichment-research.md` (the sourced replacements). It turns both into one
number per lesson: where it stands now, and where it lands if the planned
changes get built.

## How the score works

Not a formula. It's a judgement call, on the same basis as the resource
scores in `enrichment-research.md`. Each lesson sits in a band based on what
a pupil actually *does* in the deck, not just what the deck *tells* them.

| Band | What it means |
|---|---|
| 80–100 | A checked, hands-on activity is embedded in the deck itself, reveal-card load is low, pupils get real feedback on whether they're right |
| 60–79 | A checked activity exists, but it's either a late addition rather than core to the lesson, or a strong external resource with its own built-in check |
| 40–59 | Something exists for pupils to do: a worksheet, a demo, a printed sort. It isn't checked or paced inside the deck |
| 20–39 | Mostly or entirely tap-to-reveal cards. The real work is a printed sheet the deck never touches |

Current-state numbers come straight off the counts in `pedagogy-audit.md`:
slides, reveal cards, whether a Check-button activity already exists.
Projected numbers factor in the resource or build recommended for that
lesson in `enrichment-research.md`, weighted down from that resource's own
score wherever slotting it in doesn't solve the *whole* lesson's problem. An
honest gap stays a gap, and still costs points.

## Overall

| | Current | Projected |
|---|---|---|
| **All 18 lessons, average** | **42.2 / 100** | **76.8 / 100** |

The gap isn't the content. The audit already found the factual content and
the worksheets themselves fine. Fourteen of eighteen lessons never check
inside the deck whether a pupil did the thinking; they tell them the answer
and move on. Every planned change above either embeds a checked activity the
lesson currently lacks, or replaces a watch-only demo with something a pupil
has to actually do and get feedback on.

Two lessons still land under 70 projected: Y1/2 Lesson 5, and Y3/4 Lesson 3.
Both are flagged honestly in `enrichment-research.md` as places where no
strong free resource exists. The projected score there reflects a partial
fix, not a solved one.

Flipbook practice

Trace a flipbook that works first time. Then draw one with a little less help each time.

Why do flipbooks go wrong?

It jumps

You moved too far between two pages. Move a tiny bit.

It changes size

The drawing grew or shrank. Keep it the same each page.

It takes ages

Thirty pages is a lot. Start with twelve.

Twelve pages, each a tiny bit different

SPEED

Slow, and you can see it flicker from page to page.

Faster, and your eyes join the pages up into one movement.

Four steps, less help each time

STEP 1Trace the waveThe body stays the same. Only the arm moves a little each page
STEP 2Trace the bounceIt speeds up as it falls, slows as it rises and squashes when it lands
STEP 3Trace the UFOA UFO beams up a stick person. He stretches when he is pulled up fast
STEP 4Your own ideaBlank pages with a ground line. You decide what moves

Make your flipbook

1Cut along the dashed lines
2Put the pages in order, with 1 on top
3Trace each page in order. Hold it up to a window to see the page before
4Line the pages up, clip the left side and flick

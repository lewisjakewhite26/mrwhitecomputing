Getting better results · 10 min

Refining your search

Speech marks “ ”

black labrador dog finds pages with those words anywhere.

"black labrador dog" finds only that exact phrase, in that exact order.

Minus symbol −

jaguar mixes the animal with the car.

jaguar -car removes every result that mentions “car”, leaving mostly the animal.
